If you are looking for <strong>performance-based and cost effective influencer marketing</strong>, influencer seeding is one of the most powerful strategies available. This approach allows your brand to send products directly to carefully selected influencers who genuinely connect with your target audience. By gifting products instead of paying large upfront fees, you create authentic brand experiences that generate organic buzz, reviews, and user generated content.<br /><br />Our team handles the full process for you including campaign strategy, influencer selection, logistics, posting and reporting. This ensures your brand gets exposure through the right voices, while building a library of content that can be reused across paid ads, social channels and more.<br />‍<br />
