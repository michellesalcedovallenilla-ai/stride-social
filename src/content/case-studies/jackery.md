<p><strong>Introduction: </strong>Jackery’s influencer campaign highlighted their portable power solutions, driving significant brand awareness and engagement across niche as well as broad audiences.</p><p><strong>Client Background: </strong>Jackery is a leading company in the portable power generation and power banks industry. Best known for providing reliable and original products for your on-the-go power needs.</p><p><strong>Objectives: </strong>The foremost objectives of this campaign were to demonstrate the various use cases of Jackery’s products and to increase brand awareness among a targeted niche audience, but also for a broader audience too.</p><p><strong>Execution: </strong>Stride partnered with a mix of product review creators to effectively reach both niche audiences, where Jackery’s products are most aligned. A wider audience was also targeted to build overall brand awareness. The influencers created engaging and informative content showcasing real-life applications of Jackery’s portable power solutions, emphasising their versatility and reliability.</p><p><strong>Results: </strong>The campaign went better an expected, leading to multiple ongoing collaborations with Jackery. It generated over 5 million views, 400,000 likes, and 34,000 shares, significantly boosting brand visibility and interest in Jackery’s portable power products.</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
