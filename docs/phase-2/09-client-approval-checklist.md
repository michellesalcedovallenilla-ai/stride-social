# 09 — Client approval checklist

Items the client needs to confirm, supply or approve. **Critical** items block Gate 1 or Gate 2 of the [roadmap](08-roadmap.md); the rest can arrive during Phase 3 (the affected sections stay as placeholders or hidden until then). Question numbers refer to [02-requirements-analysis](02-requirements-analysis.md) §3.

## A. The brief itself

- [ ] **Critical** — Send PDF **sections 1 and 2** and the **rest of section 10** (Q22).
- [ ] Approve this Phase 2 planning package as the basis for development.

## B. Facts and claims

- [ ] **Critical** — Founding year to use everywhere: 2020, 2021 or 2022 (Q1).
- [ ] **Critical** — Number of represented creators ("600+"?) and the public roster scope: managed only, or managed + UGC partners (Q2, Q9).
- [ ] **Critical** — Verified team locations, operating markets and offices, if any (Q3).
- [ ] Keep, update or retire existing statistics: "2 billion combined followers", "4000+ campaigns", "$5M+ paid", "21 years experience" (Q7).
- [ ] Legal entity and company details for the footer and legal pages (Q15).

## C. Brand and design

- [ ] **Critical** — Approve the creative direction and design system: palette, Talent peach and Education sun accents, type pairing (Q19, Q20).
- [ ] Choose free fonts (recommended) or license a premium display face (Q19).
- [ ] Provide a **parent "Stride" logo lockup** and division lockups (Stride Social / Talent / Education), or approve us drafting options for review.
- [ ] Domain strategy: stay on stride-social.com, or adopt a group domain (Q23).

## D. Assets (photography, video, logos)

- [ ] **Critical** — Confirm whether a photo/video library exists, whether a shoot is planned, and who owns the rights (Q18).
- [ ] Hero montage footage (group, Social, Talent).
- [ ] Signed-creator portraits with consent (Three Sides panel, Talent hero, roster).
- [ ] Education imagery: sessions, filming, community.
- [ ] Natural team photography (People section, Careers).
- [ ] Approved brand logo list plus vector files: Samsung, Adobe, P&G, Govee, XGIMI, Anker, Temu, NordVPN, plus any of the 20 existing (Q5).
- [ ] Sponsorship photography and details (Q12).

## E. Work and results

- [ ] **Critical** — Samsung and Barsys campaign material: brief, strategy, execution, imagery, results (Q6).
- [ ] Verified results (with source) for Laifen, Govee, XGIMI and any other case studies to feature (Q6).
- [ ] Industry and service tags for each case study, especially Trades & Construction and Sport examples.
- [ ] Testimonials approved for reuse, plus new brand and creator quotes with consent.

## F. Services and divisions

- [ ] Confirm the 7 Stride Social services and approve the service page copy (we draft).
- [ ] Decide on services not in the new list: mobile game & app ambassadors, social media management, affiliate programme (Q8).
- [ ] Talent divisions: launch Sport, Travel, Podcasts, Music, Speakers as full pages or as filtered roster views? Which are populated today? (Q24)
- [ ] Creator data for every public profile: country, main category, divisions, platforms, selected work, consent (Q10).
- [ ] Approve the category mapping from current tags (Lifestyle, Entertainment, Tech reviews, Amazon finds…) to the new filters.

## G. Stride Education

- [ ] **Critical** — Which learning topics and experience items are available now vs planned (Q11).
- [ ] Format: cohorts, on-demand, calls, mentoring. Pricing (free/paid). Where enrollment happens (Q11).
- [ ] The final CTA route: enquiry, waitlist or course discovery (Q11).
- [ ] Creator or student success stories, when available.

## H. Pages and copy

- [ ] Mission and values for About.
- [ ] Verified milestones for the History timeline (reconcile with the founding year).
- [ ] Team bios (optional) and departments.
- [ ] Careers culture copy and application route.
- [ ] Approve drafted copy, page by page (all drafts are marked in content files).
- [ ] Navigation: keep "Our Group" and "About" as separate items, or merge (Q4).

## I. Forms, data and privacy

- [ ] **Critical** — Destinations for each form (brand, talent application, education, general, careers): inbox addresses and/or CRM (Q13).
- [ ] Budget ranges for the brand form; markets list for the "Market" field.
- [ ] Keep the Typeform for creator sign-up, or move to the native application form?
- [ ] Analytics and marketing tags to keep (GA4, GTM, Google Ads); cookieless analytics alternative; Tidio chat (Q14).
- [ ] **Critical before launch** — Privacy policy, cookie policy and terms text (Q15).

## J. Technical and launch

- [ ] **Critical** — Hosting provider and account owner; GitHub organisation for the repository (Q21).
- [ ] Media storage approach: Git LFS or an external bucket/video host.
- [ ] **Critical** — Approve URL changes and redirects: `/case-studies` → `/work`, `/blog` → `/insights`, `/creators` → `/talent/roster` (Q16).
- [ ] Chinese site (`/zh`): keep for a future translation, or redirect at launch (Q17).
- [ ] Who controls DNS for stride-social.com, and the target launch window (switch from Webflow only on explicit approval).
- [ ] Google Search Console access for the new sitemap and redirect monitoring.
- [ ] Reviewers who will approve content pull requests (named GitHub users).
