<p><strong>Client Background:</strong> Narwal is a leading technology company specialising in the development of groundbreaking robotic vacuum cleaners. Their products, designed to autonomously clean entire homes, offer advanced features that set them apart in the market.</p><p><strong>Objectives: </strong>Narwal partnered with Stride Social to promote the launch of their newest products, the NarWal Freo x Ultra and the NarWal x Freo x Plus. The primary goals were to raise brand awareness for these new releases and showcase their use case to a broad audience.</p><p><strong>Execution: </strong>To achieve these objectives, Stride Social implemented a campaign leveraging well-known and high-converting Amazon product review creators based in the US. These influencers were chosen for their credibility and ability to reach an audience with high purchasing power. The campaign content was distributed across TikTok and Instagram, ensuring maximum visibility.</p><p><strong>Results: </strong>The campaign generated over 3 million views, 125,000 likes, and 53,000 shares. These impressive metrics not only highlighted the effectiveness of the influencers but also significantly boosted brand awareness for Narwal’s new product releases.</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
