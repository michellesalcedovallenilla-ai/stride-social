<p><strong>Introduction: </strong>Bloom's influencer campaign seamlessly integrated their product into engaging content, driving massive views and sparking widespread curiosity.</p><p><strong>Client Background: </strong>Bloom is a leading gut health supplement company renowned for their signature product, Bloom Greens, which helps reduce bloating and gut pain.</p><p><strong>Objectives: </strong>The primary objective was to subtly integrate Bloom Greens into the content of our creator’s product reviews. This allowed the product to reach wider audiences without appearing as overtly promotional.</p><p><strong>Execution: </strong>Stride Social put together this campaign by discreetly mentioning Bloom Greens in every other shot of the creators' videos. This approach ensured that the audience would repeatedly see the product to spark interest without disrupting the natural flow of the content the audience is used to.</p><p><strong>Results: </strong>The campaign generated over 15 million views, 1 million likes, and 384,000 shares. This one really did blow up! The ongoing nature of the campaign continued to drive impressive engagement and brand visibility for Bloom, effectively reaching and resonating with a broad audience.</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
